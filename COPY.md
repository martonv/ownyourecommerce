## 2. The problem

**Label:** The problem

**H2:** Your systems disagree with each other.

**Intro:** Every tool is good at its job. The trouble is everything in between.

**Card 1: Stock is wrong somewhere.**
Fishbowl says 12. Shopify says 9. The warehouse says 7. Someone reconciles it by hand every week, and you still oversell.

**Card 2: Sales can't see orders.**
HubSpot knows the deal closed but not that the order is stuck in production. Your reps find out when the customer calls.

**Card 3: Every sync is something that breaks.**
Connectors, Zaps and CSV exports hold it all together until an update breaks one and nobody notices for a week.

**Closing line:** You're paying for three subscriptions, the connectors between them, and the hours your team spends fixing what falls through.

## 10. FAQ (7 questions)

**Do we have to leave Shopify?**
No. Many clients keep Shopify as the storefront and replace what sits behind it: Fishbowl, HubSpot and the connectors. We'll recommend whichever is cheaper and simpler for you.

**Who owns the code and data?**
You do. You get the source code, the database and documentation. If we disappeared tomorrow, another developer could pick it up.

**What does it cost after launch?**
Hosting, billed at cost. Ongoing development is optional: a monthly retainer or project by project.

**How do you migrate from Fishbowl?**
We export items, locations, bills of materials, open orders and history, then run both systems side by side until the counts match. You don't switch over until they do.

**Can we keep HubSpot for marketing?**
Yes. We can replace the CRM side and keep HubSpot for email and marketing, with a two-way sync for contacts.

**How long does it take?**
Most consolidation projects take 8–16 weeks. You'll see working software in the first few weeks and every week after.

**Is the AI going to make things up?**
The AI features answer only from your own data (catalog, stock, orders, policies) and hand off to a person when they aren't sure. They run on your own AI provider account with no markup.
